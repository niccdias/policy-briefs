title: "H.R.28 × Security – Personal"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025. Judged as of January 14, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Security – Personal. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to avoid disease and protect their health
- to be personally safe and secure
- to avoid anything dangerous

*Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.
