**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://web.archive.org/web/20260603151225/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28)), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025. Judged as of January 14, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid disease and protect their health. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be personally safe and secure. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid anything dangerous. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?

Threats against girls who are not transgender and their families make this a safety concern in ordinary school sports, giving [NAME] a reason to oppose it.</li></ul></td><td><p><span class="lbl">Response</span> The Utah incident shows harm from public accusations, but does not establish that H.R.28 would increase public exposure or threats.</p><ul><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> Sex already mattered to eligibility for separate teams. The existing federal rule allowed “separate teams for members of each sex where selection for such teams is based upon competitive skill or the activity involved is a contact sport.” <a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2025-01-14/title-34/subtitle-B/chapter-I/part-106/subpart-D/section-106.41" title="“34 CFR 106.41 — Athletics (historical text, January 14, 2025)”, 2025-01-14 (historical version; quoted separate-teams provision predates the 2024 amendments) (no public archived copy)">[25]</a></li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> The board dated the offending post to “February 6, 2024” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029165330/https://schools.utah.gov/board/utah/resources/BoardResolution202402.pdf" title="“Public censure of Board Member Natalie Cline”, 2024-02-14 (adopted)">[24]</a>. It creates no procedure for publicly disputing a student's sex and removes none of the duties cited by the Utah board.</li><li class="so"><span class="lbl">So</span> Changing the federal eligibility condition does not itself turn a records check into a public accusation. The PRO has not connected its example of existing misconduct to an increase in harassment or threats from this bill.</li></ul></td></tr>

As Representative Bonamici explained, “this bill applies to every student of every age in every sport the same way” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195620/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2025-01-14/html/CREC-2025-01-14-pt1-PgH126.htm" title="Bonamici, floor speech, Congressional Record, January 14, 2025">[26]</a>.</li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> Federal health guidance links youth sports with “Increased physical activity levels” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029172552/https://odphp.health.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/YSS_Report_OnePager_2020-08-31_web.pdf" title="“Benefits of Youth Sports”, 2020-09-17">[13]</a> and physical activity with “Reduced risk of cancer and diabetes” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029172552/https://odphp.health.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/YSS_Report_OnePager_2020-08-31_web.pdf" title="“Benefits of Youth Sports”, 2020-09-17">[13]</a>.</li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> A Colorado survey linked playing on three or more sports teams, versus none, to “reduced likelihood of depression” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20230827074806/https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/pits.22792" title="“Team sports participation, depression, and suicidal ideation in lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and questioning adolescents”, 2022-09-17 (first published)">[27]</a> among transgender students.</li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> Sports also supply supportive relationships.
