title: "H.R.28 × Universalism – Nature"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025. Judged as of January 14, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
