<p><strong>H.R.28 · the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:description">The bill would change Title IX, the federal law against sex discrimination in schools and colleges that receive federal money. Those schools could not let a person the bill classifies as male take part in a sports program for women or girls. The bill would decide sex “based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” The bill would still allow males to train or practice with those teams if no female loses a team place, a chance to play, a scholarship, admission to a school or another sports benefit. The bill also orders a federal study of benefits that girls could lose.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 14, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2025-01-14. A study followed teenagers at a 15-day outdoor camp. Affective beliefs are feelings about the environment.
