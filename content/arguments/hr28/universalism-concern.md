title: "H.R.28 × Universalism – Concern"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025. Judged as of January 14, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Concern. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected
- that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life
- that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

Its review of retained muscle and strength explains why lowering testosterone alone does not resolve that concern.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> H.R.28 would bar male participation in female competition at institutions receiving federal money: it forbids permitting “a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a>. That includes girls’ contact competitions where such participation is now allowed.</li><li><strong>So:</strong> The bill reduces girls’ exposure to these physical mismatches. Morgan’s wish to protect vulnerable people includes the girls facing that risk, a protection the PRO leaves out.</li></ul></td></tr>
<tr><td><p><strong>Conclusion:</strong> H.R.28 would shut a vulnerable group out of school teams, judge them by a birth label instead of as individuals, and expose all girls to body checks, so Universalism – Concern gives Morgan a reason to oppose it.</p></td><td><p><strong>Conclusion:</strong> The PRO does not establish its claimed links from H.R.28 to unequal chances, lost support, unjust treatment, or invasive checks, and leaves out the girls whose fair competition and safety the rule protects.</p></td></tr>
