**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://web.archive.org/web/20260603151225/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28)), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 14, 2025, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, January 14, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.28?

**Bill description:** H.R.28 would bar federally funded schools from allowing students who were born male — as defined by reproductive biology and genetics — to compete in sports programs designated for women or girls. It would make such participation illegal sex discrimination under Title IX.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> At schools receiving federal funds, H.R.28 would make it unlawful “to permit a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> It defines sex by birth: “sex shall be recognized based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> Male and female below follow this rule.</p><ul><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> By January 14, 2025, the 2024 federal rule had already been struck down; that day's debate acknowledged “last week's court order striking down the regulation” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195620/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2025-01-14/html/CREC-2025-01-14-pt1-PgH126.htm" title="Walberg, floor speech, Congressional Record, January 14, 2025">[2]</a>.</li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> The existing federal sports rule allowed separate teams: “a recipient may operate or sponsor separate teams for members of each sex where selection for such teams is based upon competitive skill or the activity involved is a contact sport.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029181112/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title34-vol1/pdf/CFR-2024-title34-vol1-sec106-41.pdf" title="“34 CFR § 106.41 — Athletics (2024 edition)”, 2024-07-01 (edition; PDF printed 2024-09-06)">[3]</a></li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> College rules still allowed transgender women to compete under hormone-based eligibility rules. The NCAA's May 2024 policy said: “Total serum testosterone thresholds apply to any transgender woman competing on an NCAA women’s team.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029171612/https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/inclusion/lgbtq/SSI_TransgenderSADeadlinesAndThresholdsFall.pdf" title="Mickus, Mallory, “NCAA Transgender Student-Athlete Participation Policy: Sport-Specific Testosterone Thresholds and Championship Eligibility Deadlines, Fall Sports”, 2024-05 (updated May 2024)">[4]</a></li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> H.R.28 writes its restriction into the law itself, which “is amended by adding at the end the following” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a>.</li><li class="so"><span class="lbl">So</span> H.R.28 would add an express federal birth-sex rule where existing policies allowed this participation. Covered schools would share the same rule, and an agency could not reverse it simply by changing its regulations.</li></ul></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.28?

**Bill description:** H.R.28 would bar federally funded schools from allowing students who were born male — as defined by reproductive biology and genetics — to compete in sports programs designated for women or girls. It would make such participation illegal sex discrimination under Title IX.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> H.R.28 would require federally funded schools and colleges to exclude transgender girls and women from competition in girls’ and women’s sports under Title IX, the federal law against sex discrimination in education. It bars permitting “a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> and determines sex “based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> This would end participation already allowed under some rules: the NCAA’s May 2024 policy said, “Total serum testosterone thresholds apply to any transgender woman competing on an NCAA women’s team.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029191006/https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/inclusion/lgbtq/SSI_TransgenderSADeadlinesAndThresholdsWinter.pdf" title="National Collegiate Athletic Association, “NCAA Transgender Student-Athlete Participation Policy: Sport-Specific Testosterone Thresholds and Championship Eligibility Deadlines, Winter Sports”, 2024-05 (revision date stated in the document)">[19]</a></p></div>
