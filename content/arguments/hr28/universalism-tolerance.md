**Bill:** [H.R.28](https://web.archive.org/web/20260603151225/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28)), the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 14, 2025, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, January 14, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to listen to and understand people who are different to them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to accept people even when they disagree with them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.28?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.28?

**Bill description:** H.R.28 would bar federally funded schools from allowing students who were born male — as defined by reproductive biology and genetics — to compete in sports programs designated for women or girls. It would make such participation illegal sex discrimination under Title IX.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> H.R.28 would make it a Title IX violation for education programs receiving federal aid to allow “a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> It defines sex by “a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> This would change eligibility where gender identity governed access: California law required school sports participation “consistent with his or her gender identity, irrespective of the gender listed on the pupil’s records.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260915105936/https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201320140AB1266" title="“AB-1266 Pupil rights: sex-segregated school programs and activities (2013–2014), Chapter 85”, 2013-08-12 (approved and filed)">[2]</a> The 2024 federal rule had already been struck down by January 14, 2025; reporting on that ruling noted: “The now-vacated Title IX rule did not directly address athletics” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029182049/https://www.highereddive.com/news/federal-judge-strikes-down-biden-administrations-title-ix-rule-nationwide/736975/" title="Natalie Schwartz, “Federal judge strikes down Biden administration’s Title IX rule nationwide”, 2025-01-09; updated 2025-01-10">[3]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.28?

**Bill description:** H.R.28 would bar federally funded schools from allowing students who were born male — as defined by reproductive biology and genetics — to compete in sports programs designated for women or girls. It would make such participation illegal sex discrimination under Title IX.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> H.R.28 would require schools receiving federal money to exclude students it classifies as male from girls’ and women’s sports competition. It makes it unlawful “to permit a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> It decides sex “based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> This would bar transgender girls and women and nonbinary students whom the bill classifies as male from those competitions.</p></div>
