<p><strong>H.R.28 · the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:description">The bill would change Title IX, the federal law against sex discrimination in schools and colleges that receive federal money. Those schools could not let a person the bill classifies as male take part in a sports program for women or girls. The bill would decide sex “based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” The bill would still allow males to train or practice with those teams if no female loses a team place, a chance to play, a scholarship, admission to a school or another sports benefit. The bill also orders a federal study of benefits that girls could lose.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 14, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2025-01-14. Evidence cutoff: 2026-07-17.</p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:laterEvidence">Some sources are dated after the comparison date. They are used when they bear on what the bill would have done compared with the law on that date. They do not change the law used for the comparison.</span></p><a href="https://niccdias.github.io/policy-briefs/arguments/hr28/universalism-nature">Bill and sources</a></details>
