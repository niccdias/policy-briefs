## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.28?

**Bill description:** H.R.28 would bar federally funded schools from allowing students who were born male — as defined by reproductive biology and genetics — to compete in sports programs designated for women or girls. It would make such participation illegal sex discrimination under Title IX.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> If effective on January 14, 2025, H.R.28 would make male participation in female competition a Title IX violation for education programs receiving federal aid. It would prohibit permitting “a person whose sex is male to participate in an athletic program or activity that is designated for women or girls.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> It would base sex solely on “a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a> Male training or practice would remain allowed when it “does not deprive any female of corresponding opportunities or benefits.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260603151225/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/28" title="Congressional Research Service, bill summary, January 14, 2025">[2]</a></p></div>
