<p><strong>H.R.28 · the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act of 2025</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:description">The bill would change Title IX, the federal law against sex discrimination in schools and colleges that receive federal money. The bill would decide sex “based solely on a person's reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” The bill would still allow males to train or practice with those teams if no female loses a team place, a chance to play, a scholarship, admission to a school or another sports benefit. The bill also orders a federal study of benefits that girls could lose.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_28:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 14, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2025-01-14. <details id="HR28_C01"><summary><span data-release-part="HR28_C01:title">The sports rule and its definition of sex</span></summary><p><span data-release-part="HR28_C01:statement">This rule covers a school or college that receives federal money and runs, sponsors or facilitates sports. It may not let a person whose sex is male, as the bill defines it, take part in a sports program designated for women or girls. The bill defines sex for this rule based only on reproductive biology and genetics at birth.</span></p><div data-release-part="HR28_C01:background"><p>The rule covers only programs designated for women or girls at places that receive federal money. The definition applies “For the purposes of this subsection” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029201246/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr28/BILLS-119hr28eh.htm" title="H.R.28, bill text, January 14, 2025">[1]</a>.
