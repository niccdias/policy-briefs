**Bill:** [H.R.4405](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/4405), the Epstein Files Transparency Act. Judged as of November 18, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.4405?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.4405?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.4405?
