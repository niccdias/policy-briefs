## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.4405?

**Bill description:** H.R.4405 requires the U.S. Attorney General to publicly release all unclassified Department of Justice records about Jeffrey Epstein, including flight logs, names of government officials, and records of his death in custody. It may withhold victims' personal information, active investigations, and properly classified national-security material.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Against the law in force on November 18, 2025, the bill adds a case-wide duty to publish covered Justice Department (DOJ) records about Epstein, with its listed grounds for withholding: “Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Attorney General shall, subject to subsection (b), make publicly available in a searchable and downloadable format all unclassified records, documents, communications, and investigative materials in the possession of the Department of Justice” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029202828/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr4405/BILLS-119hr4405eh.htm" title="H.R.4405, bill text, November 18, 2025">[1]</a>. The existing Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request process required a request that “reasonably describes such records” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260923012059/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title5/html/USCODE-2024-title5-partI-chap5-subchapII-sec552.htm" title="“5 U.S.C. § 552 — Public information; agency rules, opinions, orders, records, and proceedings (2024 edition)”, 2024 edition; section last amended 2016-06-30">[2]</a> and exempted some internal memos that “would not be available by law to a party other than an agency in litigation with the agency” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260923012059/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title5/html/USCODE-2024-title5-partI-chap5-subchapII-sec552.htm" title="“5 U.S.C. § 552 — Public information; agency rules, opinions, orders, records, and proceedings (2024 edition)”, 2024 edition; section last amended 2016-06-30">[2]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.4405?

**Bill description:** H.R.4405 requires the U.S. Attorney General to publicly release all unclassified Department of Justice records about Jeffrey Epstein, including flight logs, names of government officials, and records of his death in custody. It may withhold victims' personal information, active investigations, and properly classified national-security material.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> The bill would require the Justice Department to publish its Epstein and Maxwell files that are not classified as national-security secrets within 30 days of enactment: “Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029202828/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr4405/BILLS-119hr4405eh.htm" title="H.R.4405, bill text, November 18, 2025">[1]</a>. It orders officials to “make publicly available in a searchable and downloadable format all unclassified records, documents, communications, and investigative materials in the possession of the Department of Justice” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029202828/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr4405/BILLS-119hr4405eh.htm" title="H.R.4405, bill text, November 18, 2025">[1]</a>, subject to its listed exceptions.</p></div>
