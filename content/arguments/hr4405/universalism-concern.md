title: "H.R.4405 × Universalism – Concern"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.4405](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/4405), the Epstein Files Transparency Act. Judged as of November 18, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Concern. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected
- that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life
- that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose H.R.4405?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.
