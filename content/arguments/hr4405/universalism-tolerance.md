**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.4405?
