## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.4405?

**Bill description:** H.R.4405 requires the U.S. Attorney General to publicly release all unclassified Department of Justice records about Jeffrey Epstein, including flight logs, names of government officials, and records of his death in custody. It may withhold victims' personal information, active investigations, and properly classified national-security material.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> If enacted on November 18, 2025, the bill would require the Justice Department (DOJ) to “make publicly available in a searchable and downloadable format all unclassified records, documents, communications, and investigative materials in the possession of the Department of Justice” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029202828/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr4405/BILLS-119hr4405eh.htm" title="H.R.4405, bill text, November 18, 2025">[1]</a> concerning the listed Epstein matters, subject to specified exceptions. The deadline is “Not later than 30 days after the date of enactment of this Act” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029202828/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr4405/BILLS-119hr4405eh.htm" title="H.R.4405, bill text, November 18, 2025">[1]</a>. This adds a broad, timed legal duty to earlier disclosures: a November 18 statement reported that “the Committee has released more than 65,000 pages of records” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142725/http://fedorchak.house.gov/media/press-releases/fedorchak-votes-transparency-and-justice-epstein-victims" title="Fedorchak, press release, November 18, 2025">[2]</a>, while DOJ’s July memo had said “no further disclosure would be appropriate or warranted” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261023195024/https://www.justice.gov/opa/media/1407001/dl" title="U.S. Department of Justice and Federal Bureau of Investigation, “U.S. Department of Justice / Federal Bureau of Investigation memorandum on Jeffrey Epstein”, 2025-07-06 (release date; no date printed on the memorandum; release date identified in evidence/opinion_tracey_newsletter_concealing.txt)">[3]</a>.</p></div>
