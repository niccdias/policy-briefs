title: "H.R.131 × Tradition"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.131](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/131), the Finish the Arkansas Valley Conduit Act. Judged as of January 8, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Tradition. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking
- to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion
- to honor the traditional practices of their culture

*Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose H.R.131?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.
