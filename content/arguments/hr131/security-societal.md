<p><strong>H.R.131 · the Finish the Arkansas Valley Conduit Act</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_131:description">The bill would change how the local share of the Arkansas Valley Conduit is repaid to the federal government. The conduit is a federal drinking-water pipeline for southeastern Colorado. The Bureau of Reclamation, the federal water agency, builds its main pipe. The Secretary of the Interior leads the department that includes Reclamation. The Southeastern Colorado Water Conservancy District, a local public body, is responsible for repayment. The local share would stay at 35 percent of the pipeline's cost. Money paid during construction by anyone other than the Secretary would count toward that share. If the Secretary found financial hardship, the rest of the share could be repaid over as many as 75 years instead of 50, at half the usual interest rate. The bill would not provide construction money.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_131:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2025-07-21.
