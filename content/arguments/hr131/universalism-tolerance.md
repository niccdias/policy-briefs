<p><strong>H.R.131 · the Finish the Arkansas Valley Conduit Act</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_131:description">The bill would change how the local share of the Arkansas Valley Conduit is repaid to the federal government. The conduit is a federal drinking-water pipeline for southeastern Colorado. The Bureau of Reclamation, the federal water agency, builds its main pipe. The Secretary of the Interior leads the department that includes Reclamation. The Southeastern Colorado Water Conservancy District, a local public body, is responsible for repayment. The local share would stay at 35 percent of the pipeline's cost. Money paid during construction by anyone other than the Secretary would count toward that share. If the Secretary found financial hardship, the rest of the share could be repaid over as many as 75 years instead of 50, at half the usual interest rate. The bill would not provide construction money.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_131:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2025-07-21. Based on a showing of financial hardship, as decided by the Secretary, the remaining balance could be repaid over not more than 75 years, with simple interest at half the rate set under the existing law.</span></p><div data-release-part="HR131_C01:background"><p>The comparison is with the law in force on January 8, 2026. The lower rate and longer period depend on a showing of financial hardship, as decided by the Secretary of the Interior. The text does not use the word forgiveness, although the district's message and one news report describe the hardship clause that way (<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260317084246/https://secwcd.org/sites/default/files/home3/secwcdco/public_html/private/uploads/104/AVC%201-15-26%20Newsletter.pdf" title="Chris Woodka, “AVC Work Will Continue — Arkansas Valley Conduit Monthly Report”, 2026-01-15 (archived copy; the original page is gone)">district newsletter, January 2026</a>; <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029162403/https://coloradosun.com/2026/01/20/arkansas-valley-pipeline-funding-building/" title="Jerd Smith, “Arkansas Valley pipeline has about 3 years of cash left to keep building, despite presidential veto”, 2026-01-20">Colorado Sun, January 2026</a>). The bill gives no construction money and does not change the 35 percent share.</p><p>The Arkansas Valley Conduit is a pipeline planned to carry water from Pueblo Reservoir to 39 communities in southeastern Colorado. The Bureau of Reclamation is the federal agency that builds and manages federal water projects. It is part of the Department of the Interior, which the Secretary of the Interior leads. The Southeastern Colorado Water Conservancy District is the local public body responsible for repayment.</p><p>Existing law provides “100 percent federal construction financing, and 35 percent nonfederal repayment over a 50-year period, starting after project completion” (<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210940/https://www.congress.gov/119/crpt/hrpt187/generated/CRPT-119hrpt187.htm" title="H. Rept. 119-187, committee report, July 10, 2025">House committee report</a>). “Nonfederal” here means not paid by the federal government. Repayment must be finished “within fifty years from the time the works are first available for the delivery of water,” with interest based on what the Treasury pays when it borrows (<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210940/https://www.congress.gov/119/crpt/hrpt187/generated/CRPT-119hrpt187.htm" title="H. Rept. 119-187 print of existing law, law in force, July 10, 2025">existing law as printed in the committee report</a>). Simple interest means no interest is charged on past interest.</p></div><h3>Supporting evidence</h3><div data-release-part="HR131_C01:support"><p>The bill says the payments shall consist of “funding provided during construction from any entity other than the Secretary” and, “based on a demonstration of financial hardship, as determined by the Secretary, repayment of the balance not covered under subparagraph (A) for a period of not more than 75 years with simple interest at a rate that is equal to 50 percent of the interest rate determined by the Secretary of the Treasury under section 2(c)” (<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029192254/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr131/BILLS-119hr131eh.htm" title="H.R.131, bill text, July 21, 2025">bill text</a>).</p></div><h3>Response</h3><div data-release-part="HR131_C01:response"></div><details><summary>Evidence about size, timing and reach</summary><div data-release-part="HR131_C01:strength"></div></details></details>
