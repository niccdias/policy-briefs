**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, July 21, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.131?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.131?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.131?

**Bill description:** H.R.131 would have reduced what communities in Colorado must repay the federal government for building the Arkansas Valley Conduit, a federal water pipeline. It would have lowered the interest they owe and extended their repayment period.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> H.R.131 would ease repayment of the unpaid part of the communities’ construction share. It allows, “based on a demonstration of financial hardship, as determined by the Secretary, repayment of the balance not covered under subparagraph (A) for a period of not more than 75 years with simple interest at a rate that is equal to 50 percent of the interest rate determined by the Secretary of the Treasury” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029192254/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr131/BILLS-119hr131eh.htm" title="H.R.131, bill text, July 21, 2025">[1]</a>. Current law requires repayment “within fifty years from the time the works are first available for the delivery of water” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210940/https://www.congress.gov/119/crpt/hrpt187/generated/CRPT-119hrpt187.htm" title="H. Rept. 119-187 print of existing law, law in force, July 10, 2025">[2]</a>. The local share remains 35%: “payment in an amount equal to 35 percent of the cost of the conduit” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029192254/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr131/BILLS-119hr131eh.htm" title="H.R.131, bill text, July 21, 2025">[1]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.131?

**Bill description:** H.R.131 would have reduced what communities in Colorado must repay the federal government for building the Arkansas Valley Conduit, a federal water pipeline. It would have lowered the interest they owe and extended their repayment period.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> H.R.131 makes federal financing for this project cheaper and longer. Current law requires “35 percent nonfederal repayment over a 50-year period” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210940/https://www.congress.gov/119/crpt/hrpt187/generated/CRPT-119hrpt187.htm" title="H. Rept. 119-187, committee report, July 10, 2025">[2]</a>. The bill keeps the 35% share but changes repayment of the balance left after construction contributions. After “a demonstration of financial hardship, as determined by the Secretary” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029192254/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr131/BILLS-119hr131eh.htm" title="H.R.131, bill text, July 21, 2025">[1]</a>, it allows repayment “for a period of not more than 75 years with simple interest at a rate that is equal to 50 percent of the interest rate determined by the Secretary of the Treasury under section 2(c)” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029192254/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr131/BILLS-119hr131eh.htm" title="H.R.131, bill text, July 21, 2025">[1]</a>.</p></div>
