**Bill:** [H.R.5408](https://web.archive.org/web/20260618183816/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408)), the Faster Labor Contracts Act.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, June 9, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to honor the traditional practices of their culture. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?

**Reference:** Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference. Tradition here concerns preserving, observing or passing down identifiable inherited customs, religious practices and ways of life, valued for their continuity with the past. Religious endorsement of a general moral goal alone does not establish that connection.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.5408?

**Bill description:** H.R.5408 would require an employer to begin bargaining soon after its workers vote to form a union. If the two sides cannot agree on a first contract, the dispute may proceed to federal mediation and, if mediation fails, to an arbitration panel whose decision would be binding.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> The bill gives newly unionized workers a way to reach a first contract both sides must follow. Under the law in force on June 9, 2026, employers must bargain, but the law “does not impose any deadline or mandate that the parties achieve a collective bargaining agreement.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260307172802/https://www.heritage.org/government-regulation/report/labor-law-reform-part-1-diagnosing-the-issues-exploring-current" title="Heritage Foundation, “Labor Law Reform Part 1: Diagnosing the Issues, Exploring Current Proposals | The Heritage Foundation”, October 9, 2025">[1]</a> After bargaining and mediation (help from a third party to reach agreement) fail, the bill requires a panel to settle the dispute: “A majority of the 3-person arbitration panel shall render a decision settling the dispute and such decision shall be binding upon the parties for a period of 2 years, unless amended during such period by written consent of the parties.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029203943/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a> It also requires employers “to maintain current wages, hours, and terms and conditions of employment pending an agreement” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029203943/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.5408?

**Bill description:** H.R.5408 would require an employer to begin bargaining soon after its workers vote to form a union. If the two sides cannot agree on a first contract, the dispute may proceed to federal mediation and, if mediation fails, to an arbitration panel whose decision would be binding.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> The bill requires a binding first contract when talks and mediation fail. After 90 days of bargaining, either side may request mediation. After 30 days from that request, subject to agreed extensions, the dispute goes to a panel: “A majority of the 3-person arbitration panel shall render a decision settling the dispute and such decision shall be binding upon the parties for a period of 2 years, unless amended during such period by written consent of the parties.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029203943/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a> Current law “does not impose any deadline or mandate that the parties achieve a collective bargaining agreement.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260307172802/https://www.heritage.org/government-regulation/report/labor-law-reform-part-1-diagnosing-the-issues-exploring-current" title="Heritage Foundation, “Labor Law Reform Part 1: Diagnosing the Issues, Exploring Current Proposals | The Heritage Foundation”, October 9, 2025">[1]</a></p><ul><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> First-contract delays weaken new unions: “delay frustrates workers and undermines their union.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260801204348/https://www.epi.org/blog/the-missing-piece-in-the-senate-committee-hearing-on-the-challenges-facing-newly-unionized-workers/" title="Economic Policy href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029193814/https://www.seiu.org/docs/2024SEIUConstitution&amp;BylawsEnglish.pdf" title="“SEIU 2024 Constitution and Bylaws”, 2024 (adopted at the 2024 SEIU requires local payments “per member per month on all dues received by the Local Union.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029193814/https://www.seiu.org/docs/2024SEIUConstitution&amp;BylawsEnglish.pdf" title="“SEIU 2024 Constitution and Bylaws”, 2024 (adopted at the 2024 convention)">[21]</a> It assigns part to “political education and political action purposes” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029193814/https://www.seiu.org/docs/2024SEIUConstitution&amp;BylawsEnglish.pdf" title="“SEIU 2024 Constitution and Bylaws”, 2024 (adopted at the 2024 convention)">[21]</a>.
