title: "H.R.5408 × Tradition"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.5408](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408), the Faster Labor Contracts Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Tradition. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking
- to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion
- to honor the traditional practices of their culture

*Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

A later law escapes it only if it “explicitly excludes such application by reference to this chapter” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21B.htm" title="United States Congress / U.S. Government Publishing Office, “United States Code, Title 42, Chapter 21B: Religious Freedom Restoration”, 2023 (2023 United States Code edition; quoted provisions enacted November 16, 1993, and amended September 22, 2000)">[22]</a>. H.R.5408 does not. RFRA's “government” includes any “official (or other person acting under color of law) of the United States” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap21B.htm" title="United States Congress / U.S. Government Publishing Office, “United States Code, Title 42, Chapter 21B: Religious Freedom Restoration”, 2023 (2023 United States Code edition; quoted provisions enacted November 16, 1993, and amended September 22, 2000)">[22]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> Even the bill's critics treat a panel's terms as action by the government itself: “the government (through a government arbitrator) imposing terms on private parties constitutes state action” <a href="https://www.heritage.org/government-regulation/report/labor-law-reform-part-1-diagnosing-the-issues-exploring-current" title="Heritage Foundation, “Labor Law Reform Part 1: Diagnosing the Issues, Exploring Current Proposals | The Heritage Foundation”, October 9, 2025">[1]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> Courts already use RFRA to block these exact benefit demands. Christian family business owners who “have sincere Christian beliefs that life begins at conception” objected to covering drugs they believed end a life after conception. The Supreme Court ruled for them: “As applied to closely held corporations, the HHS regulations imposing the contraceptive mandate violate RFRA.” <a href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/13-354" title="Supreme Court of the United States (syllabus prepared by the Reporter of Decisions); text published by Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School, “Burwell v. Hobby Lobby Stores, Inc., 573 U.S. 682 (2014): Syllabus”, 2014-06-30">[23]</a></li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The Christian Employers Alliance itself won under RFRA in 2024. A federal court held that requiring its members to cover gender-transition procedures “violates their sincerely held religious beliefs without satisfying strict scrutiny under RFRA” <a href="https://www.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.ndd.47703/gov.uscourts.ndd.47703.82.0_1.pdf" title="United States District Court for the District of North Dakota (Daniel M. Traynor, District Judge); hosted by CourtListener (Free Law Project), “Christian Employers Alliance v. United States Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, et al., Case No. 1:21-cv-195, Order Granting Motion for Partial Summary Judgment (Doc. No. 82)”, 2024-03-04">[24]</a>, meaning the government failed RFRA's strict test.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> Many of the most religious workplaces are outside the labor law the bill changes. For Catholic schools run by the Church, “the courts have exempted these schools from the jurisdiction of the National Labor Relations Act” <a href="https://catholiclabor.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GES-Labor-Report-2025.pdf" title="Catholic Labor Network (Clayton Sinyai), “Gaudium et Spes Labor Report, 2025: Catholic Institutions with Employee Unions”, 2025 (Annual Report 2025; file uploaded August 2025)">[18]</a>.</li><li><strong>So:</strong> The PRO's risk needs a panel to impose terms that courts have already blocked when the government demanded them directly. A union's model clause and a panel majority cannot override RFRA. The PRO shows no real threat to Christian practice.</li></ul></td></tr>
<tr><td><p><strong>Point 2:</strong> The bill cuts against the Christian tradition of protecting smaller communities from outside control.</p><ul><li><strong>Because:</strong> Catholic teaching says higher bodies should help smaller ones keep their own roles: “a community of a higher order should not interfere in the internal life of a community of a lower order, depriving the latter of its functions, but rather should support it in case of need” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_two/article_1/the_person_and_society.html" title="Catechism of the Catholic Church; Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana (vatican.va), “Catechism of the Catholic Church, Part Three, Section One, Chapter Two, Article 1: The Person and Society (paragraphs 1878-1885)”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism; text as published on vatican.va)">[25]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The Manhattan Declaration defends the “intermediate structures of society” as “the essential buffer against the overweening authority of the state” <a href="https://manhattandeclaration.org/" title="Manhattan Declaration; drafting committee Robert George, Timothy George, and Chuck Colson, “Manhattan Declaration: A Call of Christian Conscience”, 2009-11-20 (release date stated in the declaration; drafted 2009-10-20)">[21]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The bill's deadlines apply without a finding of bad-faith bargaining. More time depends on agreement by both sides: “or such additional period as the parties may agree upon” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> If panel seats remain unfilled, the federal agency fills them: “the Service shall designate any members not selected by the individual or labor organization or by the employer” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>.</li><li><strong>So:</strong> The bill turns outside help into power to dictate workplace terms, even if one side wants to keep negotiating. Morgan has a religious reason to resist that transfer of the final say.</li></ul></td><td><p><strong>Response:</strong> The Christian teaching the PRO cites asks for outside help when smaller groups cannot settle things alone, and the bill offers help of that kind.</p><ul><li><strong>Because:</strong> The same Catechism passage says a higher body “should support it in case of need and help to co-ordinate its activity with the activities of the rest of society, always with a view to the common good” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_two/article_1/the_person_and_society.html" title="Catechism of the Catholic Church; Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana (vatican.va), “Catechism of the Catholic Church, Part Three, Section One, Chapter Two, Article 1: The Person and Society (paragraphs 1878-1885)”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism; text as published on vatican.va)">[25]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The Catechism names public authorities as a fitting partner in labor talks. Conflicts should be eased “by negotiation that respects the rights and duties of each social partner” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_7/iv_economic_activity_and_social_justice.html" title="Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana, “Catechism of the Catholic Church: IV. Economic Activity and Social Justice, paragraphs 2426–2436”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism)">[26]</a>, including “public authorities when appropriate” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_7/iv_economic_activity_and_social_justice.html" title="Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana, “Catechism of the Catholic Church: IV. Economic Activity and Social Justice, paragraphs 2426–2436”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism)">[26]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The need is real. The panel acts only after bargaining and mediation fail, when “the Service is not able to bring the parties to agreement by conciliation” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>. Congress found that employers use delay to “sap labor organization resolve and secure more favorable terms for the employer” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> A new union is itself one of the smaller groups this teaching protects: “the creation of voluntary associations and institutions must be encouraged” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_two/article_1/the_person_and_society.html" title="Catechism of the Catholic Church; Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana (vatican.va), “Catechism of the Catholic Church, Part Three, Section One, Chapter Two, Article 1: The Person and Society (paragraphs 1878-1885)”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism; text as published on vatican.va)">[25]</a>. Endless delay wears it down.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The two sides, not the government, pick the panel: “one member selected by the individual or labor organization, one member selected by the employer, and one neutral member mutually agreed to by the parties” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>. The agency fills a seat only if a side fails to choose within 14 days. The sides can also replace the result by a deal: “unless amended during such period by written consent of the parties” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[2]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The PRO's Manhattan Declaration quote is about something else. It defends religious institutions against limits on “the ability to hire people of one's own faith or conscientious moral convictions for religious institutions” <a href="https://manhattandeclaration.org/" title="Manhattan Declaration; drafting committee Robert George, Timothy George, and Chuck Colson, “Manhattan Declaration: A Call of Christian Conscience”, 2009-11-20 (release date stated in the declaration; drafted 2009-10-20)">[21]</a>. The bill does not touch religious hiring.</li><li><strong>So:</strong> A panel the two sides choose, used only after talks and mediation fail, is help “in case of need” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_two/article_1/the_person_and_society.html" title="Catechism of the Catholic Church; Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana (vatican.va), “Catechism of the Catholic Church, Part Three, Section One, Chapter Two, Article 1: The Person and Society (paragraphs 1878-1885)”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism; text as published on vatican.va)">[25]</a>. It is not the takeover the teaching warns against.</li></ul></td></tr>
<tr><td><p><strong>Point 3:</strong> The bill breaks a roughly 90-year American practice of leaving union contract terms to the two sides.</p><ul><li><strong>Because:</strong> The Supreme Court traced the 1935 labor law to “private bargaining under governmental supervision of the procedure alone, without any official compulsion over the actual terms of the contract” <a href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/397/99" title="Supreme Court of the United States (opinion by Mr. Justice Black); text published by Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School, “H. K. Porter Company, Inc., etc., Petitioner, v. L. 91–375, August 12, 1970)">[28]</a>. If talks with a new union yield no contract within 180 days, “an arbitration board shall be established to provide conclusive and binding arbitration” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2000-title39/html/USCODE-2000-title39-partII-chap12-sec1207.htm" title="United States Congress / U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo), “United States Code, Title 39, Section 1207: Labor disputes (2000 edition)”, 2000 (2000 edition of the U.S. Code; section as enacted by Pub. L. 91–375, August 12, 1970)">[28]</a>. The board is built like the bill's panel, with one member “selected by the Postal Service, 1 by the bargaining representative of the employees, and the third by the 2 thus selected” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2000-title39/html/USCODE-2000-title39-partII-chap12-sec1207.htm" title="United States Congress / U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo), “United States Code, Title 39, Section 1207: Labor disputes (2000 edition)”, 2000 (2000 edition of the U.S. Code; section as enacted by Pub. L. 91–375, August 12, 1970)">[28]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The rule still stood in the 2023 code: “Decisions of the arbitration board shall be conclusive and binding upon the parties.” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title39/html/USCODE-2023-title39-partII-chap12-sec1207.htm" title="United States Congress / U.S. Government Publishing Office (govinfo), “United States Code, Title 39, Section 1207: Labor disputes (2023 edition)”, 2023 (2023 edition of the U.S. Code; section enacted August 12, 1970, amended December 20, 2006)">[29]</a></li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The PRO's own Supreme Court case left this choice to Congress: “it is the job of Congress, not the Board or the courts, to decide when and if it is necessary to allow governmental review of proposals for collective-bargaining agreements and compulsory submission to one side's demands” <a href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/397/99" title="Supreme Court of the United States (opinion by Mr. Justice Black); text published by Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School, “H. K. Porter Company, Inc., etc., Petitioner, v. National Labor Relations Board et al., 397 U.S. 99 (1970)”, 1970-03-02">[27]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> Christian teaching holds that consent alone does not make pay just: “Agreement between the parties is not sufficient to justify morally the amount to be received in wages.” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_7/iv_economic_activity_and_social_justice.html" title="Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana, “Catechism of the Catholic Church: IV. Economic Activity and Social Justice, paragraphs 2426–2436”, 1997 (second edition of the Catechism)">[26]</a> It asks that work support a family: “Everyone should be able to draw from work the means of providing for his life and that of his family, and of serving the human community.” <a href="https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_7/iv_economic_activity_and_social_justice.html" title="Holy See / Libreria Editrice Vaticana, “Catechism of the Catholic Church: IV. The PRO shows an old legal rule, not a custom of Morgan's faith or culture that the bill breaks.</li></ul></td></tr>
