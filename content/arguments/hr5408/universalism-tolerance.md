title: "H.R.5408 × Universalism – Tolerance"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.5408](https://web.archive.org/web/20260618183816/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408)), the Faster Labor Contracts Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to listen to and understand people who are different to them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to accept people even when they disagree with them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.5408?

<tr class="main"><td><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> As of June 9, 2026, bargaining law requires talks but “does not compel either party to agree to a proposal or require the making of a concession” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260703004621/https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title29/html/USCODE-2024-title29-chap7-subchapII-sec158.htm" title="“29 U.S.C. § 158 — Unfair labor practices (2024 U.S. Code edition)”, 2024 edition; provision last amended 1974-07-26">[1]</a>. <tr><td><p><span class="lbl">Point 1</span> It protects a continuing voice for workers whose employer opposes their choice to organize.</p><ul><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> Delay can undermine that voice.
