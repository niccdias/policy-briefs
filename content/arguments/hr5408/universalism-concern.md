<p><strong>H.R.5408 · the Faster Labor Contracts Act</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_5408:description">This bill would change U.S. labor law for workers who have newly formed a union. An employer would have to begin talks soon after the union asks. If the two sides cannot agree on a first contract, either side can ask a federal agency for mediation, which is outside help with the talks. If mediation does not produce an agreement, a three-person panel decides, and both sides must follow the panel’s decision for two years. The bill would also require current pay and working conditions to stay in place during talks and would keep an employer’s duty to bargain until workers remove the union in an election.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_5408:comparison">If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2026-06-09. Evidence cutoff: 2026-07-17.</p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_5408:laterEvidence">Some sources are dated after the comparison date. They are used when they bear on what the bill would have done compared with the law on that date. They do not change the law used for the comparison.</span></p><a href="https://niccdias.github.io/policy-briefs/arguments/hr5408/universalism-nature">Bill and sources</a></details>
