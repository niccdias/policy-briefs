**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://web.archive.org/web/20260903044902/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88)), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on October 30, 2025, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in Senate, October 30, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid disease and protect their health. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be personally safe and secure. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid anything dangerous. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?

**Reference:** Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.J.Res.88?

**Bill description:** S.J.Res.88 would have ended the national emergency President Trump declared on April 2, 2025, to tax imports from nearly every country. Ending it would have canceled the resulting tariffs: a 10% tax on most imports, with higher rates on goods from certain countries.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> If enacted on October 30, 2025, the bill would end the April 2 tariff emergency: “is terminated effective on the date of the enactment of this joint resolution.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210706/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/sjres88/BILLS-119sjres88es.htm" title="S.J.Res.88, bill text, October 30, 2025">[1]</a> Ending that emergency ends the added import taxes based on it: “Doing so would mean that the corresponding tariffs could not be applied.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260426004508/https://www.americanprogress.org/article/how-congress-can-end-trumps-trade-war/" title="Center for American Progress, “How Congress Can End Trump’s Trade War - Center for American Progress”, April 10, 2025">[2]</a> These reasons concern goods covered by that emergency under the rules in force that day. Medical devices were being taxed: the industry’s October submission refers to revenue “collected from reciprocal tariffs on medtech since April 2025” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029182714/https://www.medicaldesignandoutsourcing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/AdvaMed-comments-on-232-Investigation.pdf" title="“AdvaMed Comments on Section 232 National Security Investigation of Imports of Personal Protective Equipment, Medical Consumables, and Medical Equipment, including Devices”, 2025-10-17">[3]</a>. Medicines were exempt: “pharmaceuticals” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261011202704/https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/07/2025-06063/regulating-imports-with-a-reciprocal-tariff-to-rectify-trade-practices-that-contribute-to-large-and" title="Executive Order 14257, executive order, April 2, 2025">[4]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose S.J.Res.88?

**Bill description:** S.J.Res.88 would have ended the national emergency President Trump declared on April 2, 2025, to tax imports from nearly every country. Ending it would have canceled the resulting tariffs: a 10% tax on most imports, with higher rates on goods from certain countries.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> If it took effect on October 30, 2025, S.J.Res.88 would end the April 2 tariff emergency and the extra import taxes based on it, at their October 30 rates. The emergency “is terminated effective on the date of the enactment of this joint resolution.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210706/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/sjres88/BILLS-119sjres88es.htm" title="S.J.Res.88, bill text, October 30, 2025">[1]</a> Ending it also removes the power to adjust those taxes under that declaration: “any powers or authorities exercised by reason of said emergency shall cease to be exercised after such specified date” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260819090136/https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1622&amp;num=0&amp;edition=prelim" title="U.S. House of Representatives, Office of the Law Revision Counsel, “50 U.S.C. 1622: National emergencies”, 1985-08-16 (last amendment); displayed text includes laws in effect on 2025-05-14 (archived copy; uscode.house.gov is under maintenance)">[31]</a>.</p></div>
