<p><strong>S.J.Res.88 · a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_S_J_Res_88:description">This bill would end the national emergency President Trump declared on April 2, 2025, to tax imports from nearly every country. A tax on imports is also called a tariff. These taxes are called “reciprocal tariffs.” Ending the emergency would cancel the tariffs based on it: a 10% tax on most imports, with higher rates on goods from certain countries. The bill would not end tariffs under other laws.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_S_J_Res_88:comparison">If this bill took effect on October 30, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in Senate · 2025-10-30.
