title: "S.J.Res.88 × Security – Societal"
---

**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. Judged as of October 30, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Security – Societal. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- that their country is secure and stable
- to have a strong state that can defend its citizens
- that their country protect itself against all threats

*Read "their country" as the United States.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

## Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor S.J.Res.88?
