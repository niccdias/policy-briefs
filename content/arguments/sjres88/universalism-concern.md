title: "S.J.Res.88 × Universalism – Concern"
---

**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. Judged as of October 30, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Concern. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected
- that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life
- that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

<tr><td><p><strong>Conclusion:</strong> Because Morgan cares about protecting the vulnerable and treating strangers justly, Morgan has a reason to oppose S.J.Res.88: it would end the tariffs that were pressing other countries to protect abused workers and to shut out goods made with forced labor.</p></td><td><p><strong>Conclusion:</strong> The PRO does not establish that Morgan’s concern for vulnerable people gives a reason to oppose this bill: it leaves tools against labor abuse intact while removing a tax that puts pressure on workers’ livelihoods and poor families’ budgets.</p></td></tr>
