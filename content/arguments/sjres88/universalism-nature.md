title: "S.J.Res.88 × Universalism – Nature"
---

**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. Judged as of October 30, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Nature. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to care for nature
- to take part in activities to defend nature
- to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

<tr><td><p><strong>Main effect:</strong> The bill ends the April 2 emergency on enactment: it “is terminated effective on the date of the enactment of this joint resolution.” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/sjres88/BILLS-119sjres88es.htm" title="S.J.Res.88, bill text, October 30, 2025">[1]</a> Ending that emergency ends the extra taxes on imports based on it: this “would mean that the corresponding tariffs could not be applied” <a href="https://www.americanprogress.org/article/how-congress-can-end-trumps-trade-war/" title="Center for American Progress, “How Congress Can End Trump’s Trade War - Center for American Progress”, April 10, 2025">[2]</a>.</p></td><td><p><strong>Main effect:</strong> Not disputed.</p></td></tr>
<tr><td><p><strong>Point 1:</strong> The bill would weaken U.S. pressure to carry out new promises to protect forests, fish and wildlife.</p><ul><li><strong>Because:</strong> Malaysia’s October 26 trade deal promises action against damage to nature: “taking measures to address illegal logging, fisheries subsidies, illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing, and illegal wildlife trade” <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/10/joint-statement-on-united-states-malaysia-agreement-on-reciprocal-trade/" title="The White House, “Joint Statement on United States-Malaysia Agreement on Reciprocal Trade”, 2025-10-26">[19]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> Cambodia’s deal requires action against stolen timber: “Cambodia shall take measures to combat, and cooperate with the United States to prevent, trade in illegally harvested forest products.” <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Annex-1.pdf" title="The White House; Cambodia “shall adopt and maintain environmental protections, effectively enforce its environmental laws” <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/10/agreement-between-the-united-states-of-america-and-the-kingdom-of-cambodia-on-reciprocal-trade/" title="The White House, “Agreement Between the United States of America and the Kingdom of Cambodia on Reciprocal Trade”, 2025-10-26">[22]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> The U.S. side of the deal is only a cap: the extra U.S. tax on Cambodian goods “shall be no higher than 19 percent” <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Annex-1.pdf" title="The White House; Governments of the United States and Cambodia, “Annexes to the Agreement Between the United States of America and the Kingdom of Cambodia on Reciprocal Trade”, 2025-10-26 (annexes to the agreement signed on this date)">[20]</a>. In July 2025 it opened a case because Brazil “appears to be failing to effectively enforce laws and regulations designed to stop illegal deforestation” <a href="https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2025/july/ustr-announces-initiation-section-301-investigation-brazils-unfair-trading-practices" title="Office of the United States Trade Representative, “USTR Announces Initiation of Section 301 Investigation of Brazil’s Unfair Trading Practices”, 2025-07-15">[23]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> In April 2025 the President told the trade office to go after illegal fishing the same way. It must examine “the relevant trade practices of major seafood-producing nations, including with regard to IUU fishing” <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/04/restoring-american-seafood-competitiveness/" title="The White House (President Donald J. Trump), “Restoring American Seafood Competitiveness (Executive Order)”, 2025-04-17">[25]</a>.</li><li><strong>Because:</strong> U.S. import bans on illegal wildlife and timber also stay in force.
