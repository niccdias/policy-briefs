title: "S.J.Res.88 × Universalism – Tolerance"
---

**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://web.archive.org/web/20260903044902/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88)), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. Judged as of October 30, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to listen to and understand people who are different to them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to accept people even when they disagree with them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
