**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://web.archive.org/web/20260903044902/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88)), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on October 30, 2025, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in Senate, October 30, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to listen to and understand people who are different to them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to accept people even when they disagree with them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.J.Res.88?

**Bill description:** S.J.Res.88 would have ended the national emergency President Trump declared on April 2, 2025, to tax imports from nearly every country. Ending it would have canceled the resulting tariffs: a 10% tax on most imports, with higher rates on goods from certain countries.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Had it taken effect on October 30, 2025, the bill would have ended the April 2 emergency and the added import taxes imposed under it. The bill says the emergency “is terminated effective on the date of the enactment of this joint resolution.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029210706/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/sjres88/BILLS-119sjres88es.htm" title="S.J.Res.88, bill text, October 30, 2025">[1]</a> Analysis of ending that same emergency explains: “Doing so would mean that the corresponding tariffs could not be applied.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260426004508/https://www.americanprogress.org/article/how-congress-can-end-trumps-trade-war/" title="Center for American Progress, “How Congress Can End Trump’s Trade War - Center for American Progress”, April 10, 2025">[2]</a></p></div>
