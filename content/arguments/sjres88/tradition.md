<p><strong>S.J.Res.88 · a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_S_J_Res_88:description">This bill would end the national emergency President Trump declared on April 2, 2025, to tax imports from nearly every country. A tax on imports is also called a tariff. These taxes are called “reciprocal tariffs.” Ending the emergency would cancel the tariffs based on it: a 10% tax on most imports, with higher rates on goods from certain countries. The bill would not end tariffs under other laws.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_S_J_Res_88:comparison">If this bill took effect on October 30, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in Senate · 2025-10-30. They do not change the law used for the comparison.</span></p><a href="https://niccdias.github.io/policy-briefs/arguments/sjres88/universalism-nature">Bill and sources</a></details>
<h2>Tradition</h2><ul data-release-part="SJRES88_TR:value"><li>to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking</li><li>to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion</li><li>to honor the traditional practices of their culture</li></ul><p><span data-release-part="SJRES88_TR:reference">Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference. Tradition here concerns preserving, observing or passing down identifiable inherited customs, religious practices and ways of life, valued for their continuity with the past. Religious endorsement of a general moral goal alone does not establish that connection.</span></p>
