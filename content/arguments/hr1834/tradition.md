title: "H.R.1834 × Tradition"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.1834](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834), the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act). Judged as of January 8, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to honor the traditional practices of their culture. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.1834?

<tr class="main"><td><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Compared with the law in force on January 8, 2026, H.R.1834 restores larger tax credits—help paying for ACA health insurance—for 2026–2028. The bill makes the changes apply to “taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[2]</a> It also extends the “Rule To Allow Credit to Taxpayers Whose Household Income Exceeds 400 Percent of Poverty Line” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[2]</a>, restoring access to help above four times the poverty line. Assuming January enactment, CBO projects more people insured than under current law: “0.1 million in 2026, 3.0 million in 2027, 4.0 million in 2028” <a href="https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61999" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[1]</a>.</p></td><td><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Not disputed.</p></td></tr>
