<p><strong>H.R.1834 · the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act)</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_1834:description">The bill would bring back larger tax credits that help people pay the monthly price (premium) of health insurance they buy through Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplaces, the government-run websites for buying insurance. Sources also call these larger credits &quot;enhanced&quot; credits or subsidies. The larger credits ended on December 31, 2025. The bill would keep them through 2028, including for households with income above four times the federal poverty level. Smaller credits would continue without the bill.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_1834:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2026-01-08. Evidence cutoff: 2026-07-17.</p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_1834:laterEvidence">Some sources are dated after the comparison date. They are used when they bear on what the bill would have done compared with the law on that date. They do not change the law used for the comparison.</span></p><a href="https://niccdias.github.io/policy-briefs/arguments/hr1834/universalism-nature">Bill and sources</a></details>
<h2>Tradition</h2><ul data-release-part="HR1834_TR:value"><li>to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking</li><li>to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion</li><li>to honor the traditional practices of their culture</li></ul><p><span data-release-part="HR1834_TR:reference">Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference. Tradition here concerns preserving, observing or passing down identifiable inherited customs, religious practices and ways of life, valued for their continuity with the past. Religious endorsement of a general moral goal alone does not establish that connection.</span></p>
