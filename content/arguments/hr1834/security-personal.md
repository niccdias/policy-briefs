**Bill:** [H.R.1834](https://web.archive.org/web/20261020233134/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834)), the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act).

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, January 8, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid disease and protect their health. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be personally safe and secure. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid anything dangerous. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?

**Reference:** Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.1834?

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces. The extension would have kept these subsidies available to households earning more than four times the federal poverty level, who would otherwise have lost them.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Compared with the law in force on January 8, 2026, H.R.1834 restores larger Affordable Care Act marketplace tax credits for 2026–2028. These help pay monthly health insurance bills. The enhancements had expired: “Yet Congress allowed the enhancements to expire on January 1.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261012152111/https://www.cbpp.org/blog/setting-the-record-straight-on-premium-tax-credit-enhancements" title="Center on Budget and Policy Priorities, “Setting the Record Straight on Premium Tax Credit Enhancements | Center on Budget and Policy Priorities”, January 12, 2026">[1]</a> The bill extends them “through 2028” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195210/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[2]</a> and applies to “taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195210/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[2]</a> The Congressional Budget Office (CBO) explains that the credit “reduces out−of-pocket costs for the premiums” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260109004859/https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-01/hr1834_0.pdf" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[3]</a>.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.1834?

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces. The extension would have kept these subsidies available to households earning more than four times the federal poverty level, who would otherwise have lost them.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Restores larger Affordable Care Act (ACA) tax credits for monthly insurance payments for 2026–2028.
