title: "H.R.1834 × Universalism – Tolerance"
---

**Bill:** [H.R.1834](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834), the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act). Judged as of January 8, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to listen to and understand people who are different to them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to accept people even when they disagree with them. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
