**Bill:** [H.R.1834](https://web.archive.org/web/20261020233134/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834)), the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act). Judged as of January 8, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
