**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, January 8, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.1834?

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces. The extension would have kept these subsidies available to households earning more than four times the federal poverty level, who would otherwise have lost them.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Restore the larger Affordable Care Act (ACA) insurance tax credits for 2026–2028, lowering eligible people's insurance bills and helping them keep coverage. The enhancements “expired on December 31, 2025” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142726/http://lalota.house.gov/media/press-releases/lalota-joins-bipartisan-effort-pass-aca-extension-senate-under-deadline" title="LaLota, press release, January 8, 2026">[1]</a>. The Congressional Budget Office (CBO) says H.R.1834 “would authorize an extension through 2028 of the premium tax credit structure” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260109004859/https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-01/hr1834_0.pdf" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[2]</a>. The bill applies “to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195210/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[3]</a> The credit “reduces out−of-pocket costs for the premiums” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260109004859/https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-01/hr1834_0.pdf" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[2]</a>, and CBO expects the bill to “increase the number of people with health insurance” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260109004859/https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-01/hr1834_0.pdf" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[2]</a>.</p></div>

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces. The extension would have kept these subsidies available to households earning more than four times the federal poverty level, who would otherwise have lost them.
