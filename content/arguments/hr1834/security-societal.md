**Bill:** [H.R.1834](https://web.archive.org/web/20261020233134/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1834)), the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act).

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in House, January 8, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country is secure and stable. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to have a strong state that can defend its citizens. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country protect itself against all threats. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.1834?

**Reference:** Read "their country" as the United States.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor H.R.1834?

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces. The extension would have kept these subsidies available to households earning more than four times the federal poverty level, who would otherwise have lost them.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Restore the larger tax credits for buying Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplace insurance for 2026–2028, compared with the law in force on January 8, 2026. The bill extends the rules “through 2028” and applies them to “taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029195210/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr1834/BILLS-119hr1834eh.htm" title="H.R.1834, bill text, January 8, 2026">[1]</a> Assuming passage in January 2026, CBO estimates additional people insured: “0.1 million in 2026, 3.0 million in 2027, 4.0 million in 2028, and 1.1 million in 2029, relative to current law.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260109004859/https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-01/hr1834_0.pdf" title="Congressional Budget Office, cost estimate, January 8, 2026">[2]</a></p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose H.R.1834?

**Bill description:** H.R.1834 would have extended for three years the enhanced health insurance subsidies that help people buy private coverage through the Affordable Care Act marketplaces.
