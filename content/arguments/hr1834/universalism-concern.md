<p><strong>H.R.1834 · the House bill extending the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits (introduced as the Breaking the Gridlock Act)</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_H_R_1834:description">The bill would bring back larger tax credits that help people pay the monthly price (premium) of health insurance they buy through Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplaces, the government-run websites for buying insurance. Sources also call these larger credits &quot;enhanced&quot; credits or subsidies. The larger credits ended on December 31, 2025. The bill would keep them through 2028, including for households with income above four times the federal poverty level. Smaller credits would continue without the bill.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_H_R_1834:comparison">If this bill took effect on January 8, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in House · 2026-01-08.
