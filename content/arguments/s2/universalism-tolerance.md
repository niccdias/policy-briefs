title: "S.2 × Universalism – Tolerance"
---

**Bill:** [S.2](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Tolerance. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to be tolerant toward all kinds of people and groups
- to listen to and understand people who are different to them
- to accept people even when they disagree with them

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.2?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

This money is “In addition to amounts otherwise available” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[2]</a>.
