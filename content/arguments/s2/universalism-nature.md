title: "S.2 × Universalism – Nature"

**Bill:** [S.2](https://web.archive.org/web/20260610223619/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2)), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.2?
