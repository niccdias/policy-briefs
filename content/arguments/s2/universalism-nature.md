**Bill:** [S.2](https://web.archive.org/web/20260610223619/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2)), the Secure America Act.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Senate-engrossed, June 5, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.2?

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security. It increases the number of Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers; expands resources for detention and deportation operations; invests in border security technology; and supports other federal immigration enforcement activities.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Compared with the law in force on June 9, 2026, S.2 adds funding for Customs and Border Protection (CBP) staff, surveillance, aircraft, and boats. The money is “In addition to amounts otherwise available” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> and remains available until “September 30, 2029” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>. Section 201 funds staff and operations “in order to carry out immigration enforcement activities.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> Section 101 funds Border Patrol staff for “functions other than immigration enforcement and customs functions.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> Section 103 funds “Upgrades and procurement of border surveillance technologies along the southwest, northern, and maritime borders.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> and “Air and Marine operations' upgrading and procurement of new platforms for rapid air and marine response capabilities.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a></p></div>

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security.
