title: "S.2 × Security – Societal"
---

**Bill:** [S.2](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country is secure and stable. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to have a strong state that can defend its citizens. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country protect itself against all threats. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.2?

<tr><td><p><span class="lbl">Point 1</span> It gives security agencies steadier resources to keep trained officers ready.</p><ul><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> The April 2026 annual funding law excluded Border Patrol and ICE: it “did not include funding for the U.S. Border Patrol or U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48874/R48874.3.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report R48874, June 24, 2026">[2]</a>.</li><li class="because"><span class="lbl">Because</span> S.2 adds money available through September 2029 “to hire, pay, train, and equip Border Patrol agents and Border Patrol support personnel” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>.
