title: "S.2 × Security – Societal"

**Bill:** [S.2](https://web.archive.org/web/20260610223619/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2)), the Secure America Act.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Senate-engrossed, June 5, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country is secure and stable. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to have a strong state that can defend its citizens. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that their country protect itself against all threats. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?

**Reference:** Read "their country" as the United States.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.2?

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security. It increases the number of Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers; expands resources for detention and deportation operations; invests in border security technology; and supports other federal immigration enforcement activities.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Compared with the law in force on June 9, 2026, S.2 adds $69.545 billion to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) for border security, immigration enforcement, and related work: “In addition to amounts otherwise available” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>; “TOTAL $69.545 billion” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029215840/https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48874/R48874.3.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report R48874, June 24, 2026">[2]</a>. This adds to the 2025 law, which “included multi-year funding for several elements of DHS.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029215840/https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48874/R48874.3.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report R48874, June 24, 2026">[2]</a> The new funds remain available through September 2029: “to remain available until September 30, 2029” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>. They include $7.45 billion for Homeland Security Investigations (HSI), the criminal investigation service within Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE): “$7,450,000,000” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>. Section 102 limits that funding to “functions other than those related to Homeland Security Investigations' immigration enforcement and customs enforcement missions.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> The HSI reasons below concern its other criminal investigations and protective work.</p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose S.2?

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security. It increases the number of Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers; expands resources for detention and deportation operations; invests in border security technology; and supports other federal immigration enforcement activities.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> Compared with the law in force on June 9, 2026, S.2 adds about $69.5 billion for border and immigration agencies: “The $69.5 billion in funding would be appropriated in fiscal year (FY) 2026 but be available for use through the end of FY 2029.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029194712/https://www.americanactionforum.org/insight/the-senates-70-billion-reconciliation-package-whats-in-whats-out/" title="American Action Forum, analysis, June 5, 2026">[25]</a> Section 202 gives Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) “$31,075,000,000, to remain available until September 30, 2029” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>, “In addition to amounts otherwise available” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>.
