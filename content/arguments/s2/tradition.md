title: "S.2 × Tradition"

**Bill:** [S.2](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to honor the traditional practices of their culture. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
