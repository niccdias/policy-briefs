title: "S.2 × Universalism – Concern"
---

**Bill:** [S.2](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Universalism – Concern. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected
- that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life
- that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.2?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.

## Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor S.2?
