**Bill:** [S.2](https://web.archive.org/web/20260610223619/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2)), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that the weak and vulnerable in society be protected. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that every person in the world have equal opportunities in life. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] that everyone be treated justly, even people they don't know. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
