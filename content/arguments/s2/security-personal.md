title: "S.2 × Security – Personal"

**Bill:** [S.2](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2), the Secure America Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Security – Personal. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to avoid disease and protect their health
- to be personally safe and secure
- to avoid anything dangerous

*Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.2?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.
