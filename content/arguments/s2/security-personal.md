<p><strong>S.2 · the Secure America Act</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_S_2:description">The bill would give about $70 billion more to federal agencies, in addition to money they already have. The bill would pay for officers and staff, detention and deportation operations, border technology, and other enforcement work.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_S_2:comparison">If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Senate-engrossed · 2026-06-05.
