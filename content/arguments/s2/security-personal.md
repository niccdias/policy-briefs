**Bill:** [S.2](https://web.archive.org/web/20260610223619/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/2)), the Secure America Act.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on June 9, 2026, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Senate-engrossed, June 5, 2026. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid disease and protect their health. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to be personally safe and secure. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to avoid anything dangerous. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.2?

**Reference:** Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.2?

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security. It increases the number of Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers; expands resources for detention and deportation operations; invests in border security technology; and supports other federal immigration enforcement activities.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> S.2 adds money for existing border protection, criminal investigations, and immigration enforcement through September 2029. Its appropriations are “In addition to amounts otherwise available” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a> and “to remain available until September 30, 2029” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a>. Section 102’s investigation funds cover work outside immigration and customs enforcement: “functions other than those related to Homeland Security Investigations' immigration enforcement and customs enforcement missions.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029205704/https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/s2/BILLS-119s2es.htm" title="S.2, bill text, June 5, 2026">[1]</a></p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose S.2?

**Bill description:** S.2 provides about $70 billion through 2029 for immigration enforcement and border security. It increases the number of Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers; expands resources for detention and deportation operations; invests in border security technology; and supports other federal immigration enforcement activities.
