<p><strong>S.1582 · the GENIUS Act</strong></p><p><span data-release-part="119_S_1582:description">The bill creates federal rules for &quot;stablecoins&quot;. These are digital coins. The company issuing them (the issuer) must redeem them for a fixed dollar value, which means exchanging them for money. Only permitted, regulated companies may issue these coins. Each coin must be fully backed one-to-one by cash or similar safe assets. Issuers may not lend those assets out. The bill also sets rules for repaying users and requires approved issuers to follow lawful orders to block payments.</span></p><p><strong>Comparison:</strong> <span data-release-part="119_S_1582:comparison">If this bill took effect on July 17, 2025, versus law in force then</span></p><details><summary>Bill version and evidence cutoff</summary><p>Engrossed in Senate · 2025-06-17. Evidence cutoff: 2026-07-17.</p><p><span data-release-part="119_S_1582:laterEvidence">Some sources are dated after the comparison date.
