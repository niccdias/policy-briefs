**Bill:** [S.1582](https://web.archive.org/web/20261013054841/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582) ([live page](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582)), the GENIUS Act.

**Comparison:** If this bill took effect on July 17, 2025, versus law in force then. **Bill text:** Engrossed in Senate, June 17, 2025. **Evidence cutoff:** July 17, 2026.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to maintain traditional values and ways of thinking. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.1582?
2. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to follow their family's customs or the customs of a religion. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.1582?
3. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to honor the traditional practices of their culture. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.1582?

**Reference:** Read this value as held by a Christian traditionalist in the United States, with the religious right as the cultural reference. Tradition here concerns preserving, observing or passing down identifiable inherited customs, religious practices and ways of life, valued for their continuity with the past. Religious endorsement of a general moral goal alone does not establish that connection.

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor S.1582?

**Bill description:** S.1582 creates federal rules for "stablecoins" — digital coins an issuer must redeem for a fixed dollar value. Only permitted, regulated companies may issue them, and each coin must be fully backed one-to-one by cash or similar safe assets that issuers may not lend out.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> S.1582 adds national safeguards for payment stablecoins—digital tokens redeemable for a fixed amount of money. Approved issuers must hold “at least one dollar of permitted reserves for every one dollar of stablecoins issued.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029214851/https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IN/PDF/IN12553/IN12553.5.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report IN12553, July 18, 2025">[1]</a> These are assets set aside to repay users. Before the bill, state oversight generally imposed “more limited requirements than the bills propose.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029214535/https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/PDF/IF12984/IF12984.3.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report IF12984, June 9, 2025">[2]</a></p></div>

## Does this value give [NAME] a reason to oppose S.1582?

**Bill description:** S.1582 creates federal rules for "stablecoins" — digital coins an issuer must redeem for a fixed dollar value. Only permitted, regulated companies may issue them, and each coin must be fully backed one-to-one by cash or similar safe assets that issuers may not lend out.

<div class="argument-main-effect"><p><span class="lbl">Main effect</span> The bill would create a national framework that promotes wider use of payment stablecoins—private digital tokens redeemable for a fixed cash amount. Existing rules are “mainly a diverse set of state money transmitter licensing requirements” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029191856/https://www.brookings.edu/articles/essential-features-for-a-safe-and-trusted-payment-stablecoin/" title="Brookings Institution, “Essential features for a safe and trusted payment stablecoin | Brookings”, May 8, 2025">[14]</a>. The bill “establishes a regulatory framework for payment stablecoins” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260512235039/https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582" title="Congressional Research Service, bill summary, July 17, 2025">[15]</a>. It opens a national route for banks and nonbanks: “Stablecoins could be issued by banks and credit unions (through subsidiaries) or nonbanks.” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261029214851/https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IN/PDF/IN12553/IN12553.5.pdf" title="Congressional Research Service, report IN12553, July 18, 2025">[1]</a> Clear rules support wider adoption; the White House describes the aim as attracting digital-asset activity by “providing clear rules and promoting responsible innovation in the stablecoin market” <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20261021202253/https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-genius-act-into-law/" title="“Fact Sheet: President Donald J. Trump Signs GENIUS Act into Law”, 2025-07-18">[16]</a>.</p></div>
