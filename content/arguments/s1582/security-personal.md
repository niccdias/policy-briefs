title: "S.1582 × Security – Personal"
---

**Bill:** [S.1582](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1582), the GENIUS Act. Judged as of July 17, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Value:** Security – Personal. A person who holds this value finds these things very important:

- to avoid disease and protect their health
- to be personally safe and secure
- to avoid anything dangerous

*Read this value as held by an average American: ordinary personal health and safety risks in U.S. circumstances, without assuming a specially exposed person.*

**Survey question:** “Imagine a person named Morgan. It's very important to Morgan [statement]. Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor or oppose S.1582?” Each respondent saw one of the statements above.
