>   > [!info]- What the record shows (partly settled)
>   > Bill text: “(B) depict or contain child sexual abuse materials (CSAM) as defined under 18 U.S.C. 2256 and prohibited under 18 U.S.C. 2252-2252A;” [[sources/bill_text#q2|↗ source]]
>   >
>   > As Position 1 says, the bill text lets the Attorney General withhold child sexual abuse material "as defined under 18 U.S.C. 2256", and the Justice Department has cited that clause. 18 U.S.C. 2256 defines "child pornography" but not "child sexual abuse materials", as Position 2 says, and no cited source says whether the cross-reference still gives the authority.
