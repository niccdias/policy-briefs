**Bill:** [H.R.5408](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408), the Faster Labor Contracts Act. Judged as of June 9, 2026, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [name]. The arguments call this person Morgan.

1. Imagine a person named [name]. It's very important to [name] to care for nature. Does this value give [name] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
1. Imagine a person named [name]. It's very important to [name] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [name] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?
1. Imagine a person named [name]. It's very important to [name] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [name] a reason to favor or oppose H.R.5408?

Answer options: A reason to favor H.R.5408 / A reason to oppose H.R.5408 / Both / Neither

## Does this value give Morgan a reason to favor H.R.5408?

<tr><td><p><strong>Main effect:</strong> Compared with the law in force on June 9, 2026, the bill would give newly recognized or certified unions under the National Labor Relations Act deadlines and a binding way to settle stalled first-contract talks. Existing law says bargaining “does not compel either party to agree to a proposal or require the making of a concession” <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title29/html/USCODE-2024-title29-chap7-subchapII-sec158.htm" title="“29 U.S.C. § 158 — Unfair labor practices (2024 U.S. Code edition)”, 2024 (2024 U.S. Code edition)">[1]</a>. The bill adds “mandatory deadlines for parties negotiating an initial collective bargaining agreement (CBA) and provides for mediation and arbitration to finalize CBAs.” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5408" title="Congressional Research Service, bill summary, June 9, 2026">[2]</a> Either party could request mediation. If it fails, the bill requires referral to a panel whose decision would be “binding upon the parties for a period of 2 years” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[3]</a>. This strengthens workers’ bargaining position against delay: Congress finds that employers use delays “to sap labor organization resolve and secure more favorable terms for the employer.” <a href="https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr5408/BILLS-119hr5408eh.htm" title="H.R.5408, bill text, June 9, 2026">[3]</a></p></td><td><p><strong>Main effect:</strong> Not disputed.</p></td></tr>

Its outcome is binding: “A decision of the Board shall be final and binding upon the Company, the Union and all Employees concerned.” <a href="https://usw.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/2018_uss-usw_pm_bla_printer-2-1.pdf" title="“Agreement between United States Steel Corporation and the United Steelworkers: Production and Maintenance Employees”, 2018-09-01 (agreement date)">[4]</a></li><li><strong>So:</strong> These terms give workers information, a voice and a way to enforce environmental duties.
