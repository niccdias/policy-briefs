**Bill:** [S.J.Res.88](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-joint-resolution/88), a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. Judged as of October 30, 2025, when Congress last voted on it.

**Survey question:** Each respondent saw one of these three versions, with a randomly assigned name in place of [NAME].

1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to care for nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to take part in activities to defend nature. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
1. Imagine a person named [NAME]. It's very important to [NAME] to protect the natural environment from destruction or pollution. Does this value give [NAME] a reason to favor or oppose S.J.Res.88?
