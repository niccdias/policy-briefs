`pro_review` and `con_review` are checks of a draft inside the drafting chain. They do not replace the full quality and clarity pass.

Opus owns the final wording of project prose: arguments, responses, shared claims, evidence notes, bill descriptions, coder instructions, explanations, summaries and interface messages. Sol may supply initial drafts, source excerpts and structured research or verification findings. Opus prepares or explicitly approves the final prose; Sol must not leave its own unreviewed wording in a release. Preserve user-specified wording and verbatim source quotations exactly.

Sol reports verification issues and supporting evidence. Wording corrections, including factual or scope corrections, return to Opus for integration; Sol independently verifies the result without rewriting it. Website, survey and private-saving implementation uses the approved wording verbatim. Any new or changed prose needed during implementation returns to Opus before publication. Use the existing subscriptions and verify the effective model and effort for each assignment. Workers do not delegate further. Human reviewers retain the final argument and strength judgments.

This allocation governs subsequent work. It does not reattribute earlier deliveries, rerun a review or change published content or coding records.

## Drafting standard

The drafting prompts ([common.md](common.md) and [roles.md](roles.md)) ask for every path the evidence supports, including small and secondary effects, with the limits the evidence shows. They set no main-effect restriction and contain no Main effect section. They do not ask writers to argue at full strength or forbid concessions. They do not require a rebuttal of every PRO point. A CON either identifies a genuine defect in the actual PRO point or uses the exact marker `N. No defensible CON retained.`

Every stage applies the exact value statements and reference profiles and these boundaries:

- **Tradition:** the argument must connect the bill to an identifiable inherited custom, religious practice or way of life, valued for its continuity with the past. Religious endorsement of a general moral goal alone does not establish that connection.
- **Universalism – Tolerance:** general information or general compassion alone is insufficient.
- **Security – Societal:** an isolated harm to individuals does not become a societal harm by relabeling.

## Procedure

1. Run the four Sol 6.1 Max drafting stages from the current prompts. A launcher that hardcodes older prompts or an older model assignment must not be used.
2. Prepare a separate, blinded packet of the arguments, complete paired briefs, value statements, fixed reference profiles, bill version, comparison date, evidence cutoff and source record. Preserve the starting files and stable argument IDs. Use the current accepted content and definitions for the new assignment. Do not expose keys, manuscript judgments or coder responses.
3. Copy the current worker brief into the new worker workspace as `task.md`; do not use a historical launcher that copies a dated task file. Give Opus 5.5 at High effort that brief through the existing Claude subscription, with no further delegation. Verify effective native model and effort at the start and end using the sanitized gate; the subscription plan name is not the effort setting.
4. In one editorial pass, assess the reasoning on both sides, with particular scrutiny of responses. Then rewrite every surviving point as needed for an adult ESL coder with little or no prior knowledge of the bill, policy area, US government, law or relevant technical concepts, including sound arguments. Make the bill change, causal steps, value connection and any genuine objection easy to follow. Check each rewrite against the substantively reviewed claim. Clarity cannot repair a missing premise merely by adding reassuring language.
5. Have Sol 6.1 Max independently inspect every removal, substantive revision, merge and dependency, plus the seeded sample of retained arguments defined in [current_contract.md](current_contract.md). Sol verifies the meaning of clarity edits, with source-context checks where needed, and distinguishes clarity-only edits from changes to the argument.
6. Sol 6.1 Max conducts targeted evidence retrieval for material open questions.
7. Opus 5.5 High makes one bounded check of affected pairs and genuinely comparable defects and integrates accepted corrections. Sol independently verifies the integrated result. Do not repeat full reviews merely until the reviewer is satisfied. A material defect leads to a bounded comparable pass, and that pass crosses bills only where the defect has a shared cause.
8. Have Sol 6.1 Max verify coverage, IDs, original numbering, citation bindings, quotations, note attachments, paired responses and outer claims. Preserve originals alongside the proposed clear versions and ledger. Sol implements the separately authorized website, survey and private-saving work using Opus's integrated content only after review and verification.
9. Close every finding before publication, as set out in [Finding closure](#finding-closure).

The ESL requirement covers headings, arguments, responses, shared objections, evidence notes, bill descriptions and surrounding brief prose. Explain the background needed to follow each point, including institutional roles, the current rule and the bill's change. Each randomized point must be understandable with its own response, notes and fixed context; do not rely on another argument being read first. Use supported background from the supplied record and flag gaps for evidence review. It preserves the supplied value statements, reference profiles, survey judgment wording, uncertainty and legal scope. There is no arbitrary reading-grade or word-count target. Every changed point is recorded as Revise; its rationale identifies a clarity-only edit, a substantive edit or both. Keep remains exact original text. A completed model clarity pass does not establish human comprehension.

## Finding closure

A finding is any concern raised about release content by a writer, reviewer, verifier, the main agent or Nic. Publication is blocked until every finding is in one inventory and closed. **An audit log alone never closes a confirmed defect.** A confirmed defect cannot be deferred to a later release: it is corrected, the point is retired, or the release waits.

| Class | Meaning | How it closes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed defect | The text misstates a source, overreaches its scope, breaks a value boundary, misattributes a statement or gives coders an editorial verdict | Opus corrects it or retires the point. Sol verifies the result against the source. |
| Genuine uncertainty | An empirical or legal question is open after the source context is read | Sol investigates. Opus then qualifies the text accurately or removes the unsupported branch. Sol independently verifies the rationale. |
| Human coding judgment | Reasonable reviewers could weigh a supported argument differently | The content stays for human reviewers. Sol independently checks that this is the right class and that the page gives coders what they need. No model answer is assigned. |
| No defect | The concern fails after the context is checked | Sol independently checks the recorded reason. |
| Declared limit | A limit on what verification shows, not a claim in the content, such as untested human comprehension | It is stated openly in the release record. It cannot be used for a content finding. |

Retiring a point keeps its historical ID, its original text and any earlier answers. A point is not given new reasoning merely to save it. A correction that changes a shared claim or route needs its dependencies checked, and a change to judgment-relevant text reopens the affected judgments under the current contract.

The review questions and the strength comparison below do not change. Nothing in this process assigns a coding answer. [finding_closure_gate.md](../procedure_amendments_20261107/finding_closure_gate.md) defines the machine gate.

## Mapping notes

The main agent independently checks every merge, dependency and alternative route: is the premise used at the same scope as the local argument, and is it truly necessary for that route? Exhaustive logic tests validate a supplied map. They do not establish that its edges are right.

A human coordinator disposes of each ordinary mapping note before affected results are finalized, using one of three outcomes: "No mapping change", "Mapping revised" or "Unresolved". After a mapping correction, derived outcomes are recomputed. An argument with a viable route needs a direct human judgment. Only an argument with every route blocked derives No. A continued dispute stays Unresolved. Earlier answers are preserved. There is no dependency flag.

## Strength comparison guide

Keep the exact questions: "Does this argument establish a reason to SUPPORT [BILL]?", "Does this argument establish a reason to OPPOSE [BILL]?" and "Which direction has the stronger reason, considering all accepted arguments for this value?"

The comparison is a judgment of the overall case for each direction, based on the distinct consequences in the accepted arguments. Reviewers consider all the effects, not only the strongest one. They do not count headings, and they do not count the same consequence twice. How well the evidence supports an effect is a different question from how large the effect is. Reviewers choose "Pending — no justified comparison yet" when no comparison is justified. There is no numerical formula.

This procedure update does not rewrite the frozen releases, change the live briefs or surveys, or alter any unfinished response. A future content run must start from the accepted current material, including any later value-boundary amendments, rather than replaying an old pre-amendment packet.
